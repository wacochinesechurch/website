# Fact audit — Waco Chinese Church

**Audited 21 September 2026** against wacochinesechurch.org, its Squarespace
sitemap, the archived pre-2019 WordPress site, Google Business Profile, Yelp,
Texas Comptroller records, Texas Baptists / Baptist Standard coverage of
Emmanuel Baptist Church, ebcwaco.org, and several Chinese church directories.

This is the document the rebuild was built from. Nothing appears on the new
site that is not traceable to something below.

---

## The headline problem with the old site

Six of its nine pages were last modified in **2023**. The three touched since
were touched without their content being updated: on 21 September 2026 the
homepage still led with a **March 2024** picnic, and the page literally titled
"最新通知 / latest announcements" still advertised a **Lunar New Year**
celebration as happening "this Sunday".

That is not a content problem. It is a structural one — evergreen facts and
time-sensitive notices lived in the same undifferentiated soup, and removing
something was always a manual job somebody had to remember. The new content
model separates the two and makes expiry automatic. See `DESIGN.md` and the `README`.

## POSSIBLY OUTDATED

- **Everything on six of nine pages.** /about, /sermons, /church-life,
  /fellowship, /new-page and /giving all carry 2023 `lastmod` dates.
- **"The past 25 years… over 100 members."** Written around the church's 25th
  year (~2018). As of 2026 it would be 32–33 years. The figure is used on the
  new site *only* inside the 2018 timeline entry, framed as of that date.
- **The YouTube channel.** `@wacochinesechurchmedia1963` has published nothing
  since **10 December 2023**. The old /sermons page advertised a weekly
  "Sunday 10:45 am live stream" whose link was in fact a frozen recording of
  17 September 2023. The new site describes the channel as an archive.
- **All four serving/mission items** are dated 2023 (Underbridge Church meals,
  Operation Christmas Child Nov 2023, Jena Germany summer 2023, Dominican
  Republic summer 2023). Published on the new site as a dated record, not as
  current activity.
- **Website volunteer credits** (焦宇焰 Joy Jiao, 林建成 Matt Lin, Brandon Dahl,
  闫家驹 Justin Yan; poster design 姜玫伶 Meiling Jiang) date from a 2023-built
  footer. Students graduate. Not carried over.
- **All photography** is from September 2023.

## MISSING / REQUIRES CONFIRMATION

**Blocking — the site cannot be fully trustworthy without these**

1. **Confirmation the published times are still right.** Resolved for launch by
   the client (11:00 Sunday) and corroborated by Google, but it should be
   confirmed by someone who was there last Sunday.
2. **Is wacochinesechurch@gmail.com actually monitored, and by whom?** It is
   the site's only contact route. If that inbox is unattended, the rebuilt
   site has no working way for a visitor to reach anybody.
3. **Something genuinely current for the notice slot.** The architecture is
   built and self-expiring; it needs one real item on day one.

**Not blocking, but each one directly helps a first-time visitor**

- **Parking.** Never published. The FAQ entry exists and is hidden.
- **The age range for the children's group.** The church confirms younger
  children have their own group during the message; the exact ages it suits
  are still worth stating, since that is a parent's next question.
- **What to wear.** Never published. FAQ entry exists and is hidden.
- **When the seniors' fellowship meets.** Three of the five now have confirmed
  times (the Friday study groups) and the children's group meets during the
  Sunday message. 荣耀冠冕团契 is the remaining one with no published time.
- **A statement of faith.** None exists publicly. The section is built and
  hidden. *Not written here — this requires church approval.*
- **Who leads the church.** No pastor, elder or staff member is named anywhere
  in public material. The Leadership section is built and does not render.
- **Check payee.** /giving gives a mailing address but never says who to make
  the check out to.
- **Tax / 501(c)(3) status.** No such language anywhere. Texas Comptroller
  records show exemption responsibility beginning 2008-01-01, which is not the
  same as a published charitable-giving statement.

## Things deliberately NOT on the new site

Each of these is an intentional omission, not an oversight:

- **A phone number** — none exists; the one in directories reaches another church.
- **A statement of faith** — requires church approval.
- **Named leaders** — none are published anywhere.
- **Sermon entries** — the church has never published an indexed archive.
- **Fellowship meeting times** — never published.
- **Parking and dress code** — never published. (Childcare is now answered.)
- **Any social channel other than YouTube** — no Facebook, Instagram, WeChat,
  GroupMe or WhatsApp account could be verified as belonging to this church.
  Several similarly-named accounts belong to Chinese churches in other cities.

Every one appears on the internal checklist at **`/en/review`**, generated
from the content itself. Fix a field in the CMS and its row disappears.
